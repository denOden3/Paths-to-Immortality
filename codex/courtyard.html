## Physical location
A broad lower-mountain terrace of wooden walkways, modest tiled dormitories, communal kitchens, laundry lines, training squares, and a notice board beneath an old ginkgo tree. Water runs through stone channels to a bathhouse and small practice garden. The courtyard is humble but well maintained; beyond its roofs, upper peaks disappear behind cloud.
## Areas
- **Registration Hall:** after the third recruitment trial, records candidates' provisional arrival, examination token, and overnight accommodation; following the Day 2 Central Assembly Hall acceptance ceremony, confirms formal admission and provides orientation, daily duties, and introductory schedules. Temporary registration is not disciple status.
- **East and West Dormitories:** simple rooms assigned flexibly; do not predetermine player roommates or force the protagonists to share quarters.
- **Dining Hall & Bathhouse:** ordinary daily life, gossip, friendships, and interruptions.
- **Practice Square:** supervised basic breathing, movement, and safe sparring.
- **Ginkgo Notice Board:** lectures, work requests, disciplinary notices, and apprenticeships.
- **Lower Trail Junction:** leads west to Sword Peak, east to Verdant Pill Hall and herb terraces, north toward Talisman & Formation Hall, and southeast toward Spirit Beast Vale; authorization may be required.
## Ordinary routine
Morning bell; breakfast; assignments and introductory lessons; afternoon duties or free study; evening meal; nighttime quiet hours. The GM may vary the schedule for festivals, incidents, and individual mentors. Outer Disciples normally receive basic meals, accommodation, and entry-level teaching. Merit may earn access to materials or specialized lessons; do not introduce grinding or excessive accounting.
## Initial state
At the start of Recruitment Day 1, existing outer disciples are busy preparing beds and orientation material. Candidates completing the third Mountain Gate trial are directed here to check in at the Registration Hall, receive **temporary dormitory assignments**, and find food or rest while awaiting **Day 2 aptitude findings and formal acceptance at #central-assembly-hall**. They may use designated guest facilities but are not yet enrolled disciples. Staff should explain this distinction clearly. The area becomes the shared home base after acceptance, and dormitory arrangements can be confirmed or adjusted thereafter without forcing roommates. Its residents may be invented as needed, then promoted to canon if significant.
## Independent player scenes
Players may attend different lessons, make friends, pursue interests, or be away on branch errands. Different channel scenes occur in the same shared world; synchronize dates and major consequences with journal updates. A player's absence does not stop all other events, but never force that player's action offscreen.
