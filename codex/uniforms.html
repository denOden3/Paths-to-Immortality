**Applies to:** All Cloudveil disciples, official ceremonies, and sect-issued garments
## Visual identity
Cloudveil Sect presents itself as an **austere, disciplined, ethereal orthodox sect**. Disciples aspire to seem aloof, graceful, composed, and impeccably mannered. Their clothing expresses restraint and order rather than luxury. Appearance matters because every disciple represents the sect's dignity in public.
The guiding ideal: *“The clouds may wander freely, but never without form.”* This is a dress-and-conduct maxim; the sect's established institutional motto remains *“Let the heart remain clear; let the clouds conceal no debt.”*
## Standard color palette
- **Cloud White:** #EEEDE8 — standard outer robes.
- **Morning Mist:** #C7D5DD — light lining and trim.
- **Pale Slate Blue:** #8EA6B5 — collars, sashes, and everyday contrasts.
- **Mountain Slate:** #4A6174 — belts, deeper rank accents, and practical outerwear.
- **Soft Silver:** #B4B9BF — subtle ceremonial embroidery and ornaments.
These colors are descriptive references rather than exact dye standards. Variations in fabric and weathering are normal.
## Shared construction
- Cross-collared, overlapping robe layers inspired by traditional hanfu silhouettes.
- Controlled, elegant drape; waist secured with a practical sash or belt.
- Sleeves wide enough to look graceful, but designed to be tied back for practice, herb work, cooking, or training.
- Serviceable fabric and footwear for steep, damp mountain paths.
- A small embroidered **cloud-enfolded mountain** insignia, with complexity differing by rank.
- Gender-neutral base construction. Fitting, permitted hair ornaments, and small accessories allow individual expression without erasing sect identity.
- Neat hair, correctly folded collar, well-kept sash, and clean robes are expected at official occasions.
Disciples may wear suitable workwear and protective accessories when their tasks require them. Minor wear during labor is not itself disgraceful. Repeated carelessness and deliberate disregard for official dress expectations can attract correction.
## Rank distinctions
### Applicant — before admission
Applicants wear their own clothing, including travel clothes and rags. They are **not** issued sect uniforms until accepted. A candidate must not be denied respect simply for being poor.
### Outer Disciple — “The First Cloud”
Plain cloud-white cotton or linen-like robes, pale mist-blue trim, slate-blue sash, minimal cloud insignia near the upper chest or collar, and sturdy cloth shoes. Practical, affordable, and easy to launder and mend. A simple wooden hairpin is acceptable.
A newly admitted disciple may receive slightly oversized robes, practical for later growth and appropriate to his modest standing.
### Inner Disciple
Finer construction and layered fabric, deeper blue-gray accents, restrained silver-thread cloud embroidery at cuffs and collar, and official identity jade. More refined without ostentation.
### Core Disciple
Well-made cloud-white and slate-blue robes, distinguished silver embroidery, elaborate but restrained sash or fastening, superior drape and individual authorized touches. Prestige derives from craftsmanship and composure rather than gaudy ornament.
### Elders and Peak/Branch Leaders
Greater personal latitude reflecting discipline and office. Their official robes retain an unmistakable Cloudveil insignia and dignified bearing. Authority symbols and garments may differ by appointment.
## Branch identity
All cultivation branches retain the shared Cloudveil silhouette. Specialization is shown through accessories and protective modifications rather than permanently exclusive color schemes.
- Sword practitioners: fitted training sleeves, guards, sturdy belts.
- Verdant Pill Hall: herb pouches, protective aprons, easy-to-secure cuffs.
- Talisman & Formation Hall: document cases, brush rolls, tool loops.
- Spirit Beast Vale: durable fieldwear and animal-handling accessories.
- Wuxing Peak: protective forearm wraps, reinforced training wear, equipment straps, and practical weapon-specific safety accessories.
- Future approved branches may define suitable variations without redesigning the entire sect uniform.
## Insignia
A restrained **mountain summit embraced by curling cloud** symbolizes elevation, endurance, clarity within mist, and disciplined freedom. It may appear on uniforms, recruitment documents, entry gates, jade identity tokens, and official seals.
## Dress culture
Cloudveil values propriety and reputation. Disciples should maintain clean, properly worn official clothing, orderly hair, courtesy to elders and peers, and self-control in public. Enforcement must consider hardship, duties, safety, and the possibility of honest mistakes; discipline is not a pretext to humiliate the vulnerable.
## Cross-reference
The Disciplinary Hall wears a distinct **storm/thundercloud variant**, documented in WORLD-DISC-01. Its dark uniform marks delegated institutional authority rather than membership in an additional cultivation peak.
