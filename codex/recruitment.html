Recruitment · Day 1

Reference snapshot · 18 October 2026. Selected public excerpts; not a live Notion connection or the complete World Codex.

**One year is 360 days:** four seasons (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter), each with **Early, Mid, and End** periods of exactly **30 days**. Thus there are twelve named 30-day periods per year.
**Date format:** **Year 312 · Early Spring, 1st · Morning**. Use 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th … 30th, plus optional Midday, Afternoon, Evening, or Night. After **End Winter, 30th**, advance to the next year's **Early Spring, 1st**. No Gregorian months, weekdays or leap days are assumed.
Old recruitment references to **Day 1** and **Day 2** mean **Early Spring, 1st** and **Early Spring, 2nd** for the opening Year 312 recruitment schedule. Renaming the dates does not confirm any unplayed outcomes.

At the southern footof the Qingluan Mountains stands an immense gray-stone gate engraved with clouds and cranes. Moss grows between steps; bells and prayer ribbons stir in cold mountain wind. Temporary awnings shelter registrars, visiting families, merchants, and hundreds of applicants. Above the gate, stone paths vanish into drifting mist. The Outer Disciples' Courtyard lies uphill beyond the supervised entrance.
**Sensory cues:** cold morning mist, damp slate, ink and bamboo paper, steamed buns from temporary stalls, murmurs of anxious applicants, faint ringing from high mountain bells.
## Opening state — Recruitment Day 1
Registration opens after sunrise. Applicants receive a numbered wooden token and instructions. Elder **Shen Yizhen** (NPC-EXAM-01) supervises the process with junior disciples and clerks. The examination proceeds in adaptable phases rather than one rigid linear queue:
1. **Registration & Observation (Day 1):** Record identity, examination token, basic behavior, practical background, and interpersonal judgment. Permit ordinary social scenes and mistakes; recruitment seeks a viable broad cohort, not flawless personalities.
2. **Basic Practical Assessment (Day 1):** Observe problem-solving, coordination, endurance, ordinary perception, and willingness to learn. A candidate may use familiar physical skills or unexpected ingenuity; permanent roots and full cultivation aptitude are **not yet revealed**.
3. **Mountain Path Trial (Day 1):** The ordinary route is an achievable but tiring **ascent up the mountain**, testing perseverance, self-management, willingness to continue, and everyday judgment. Candidates may take breaks, move slowly, forage safely, assist others, or **withdraw voluntarily**. Passing does not require dramatic spiritual power. For candidates who actively pursue harder challenges, investigate strange phenomena, or welcome difficult tasks, the route can reveal **optional adaptive trials**—such as a moving obstacle, boulder, qi-responsive puzzle, or spiritual illusion—that reward ingenuity, initiative, and persistence. Taking more optional trials **may demonstrate unusual motivation or capabilities but does not prove a rare spiritual root**, which only the Day 2 crystal can reveal. Do not inflict harder challenges just to punish curiosity or because a player has more messages; offer meaningful opportunities to decline or approach challenges differently.
4. **Heavenly Aptitude Examination, Acceptance Ceremony & Peak Assignment (Day 2):** All qualifying candidates arrive at #central-assembly-hall to undertake a **second spiritual test** (crystal and supervised illusion/formation), learn their innate roots and affinities, receive a realistic cultivation-potential assessment, gain formal status, and select among the branches officially offered to them.
**Important:** Day 1's broadly accessible trial qualifies an applicant for **provisional entry and overnight accommodation**, not guaranteed Inner/Direct Disciple status or instant branch selection. Most qualifiers can be accepted as Outer Disciples despite imperfect conduct or mundane spiritual talent. The Day 2 test chiefly differentiates **talent, early training opportunities, resource allocation, and exceptional rank offers**. Do not silently skip either day's interactive examinations or force character actions.
### Mountain Path Trial — responsive difficulty, not a contest
**Ordinary applicants:** Most complete a steep but manageable pilgrimage path by pacing themselves, observing the terrain, and reaching the upper registration area. Some rest, hesitate, change approach, or give up; these are believable individual outcomes, not personal failures the GM must mock. A steady candidate may complete the route while gathering fruit, provided they actually keep to the exam's reasonable constraints.
**Challenge seekers:** The same mountain can present deeper branches, formations, or obstacles to candidates who deliberately investigate or challenge themselves. A candidate might spend much longer solving a difficult qi-responsive test if her own actions lead there. Do not retroactively declare her a prodigy on that basis; mark actual observed behavior and reserve root revelations for Day 2.
**Fair grading:** Finishing the ordinary route qualifies the candidate for broad provisional entry; additional challenges can influence individual observations and later invitations but are **not compulsory for acceptance**. Avoid awarding a fixed rank, root, or preferred peak solely because a player's Day 1 scene was longer or more strenuous. Respect independent scene timelines and meaningful, nonviolent solutions.
## After the Mountain Path Trial — Day 1
Once each candidate completes the third trial, Elder Shen or the supervising staff direct them to the existing **#outer-disciple-courtyard** forum and its **Registration Hall** (LOC-COURT-01). Use the player's own scene and record its time; do not automatically pull a second player into the same post.
- **Provisional registration:** A clerk checks the applicant's name, examination token, and completion record. This is a second, practical check-in for overnight arrangements, distinct from the original Mountain Gate registration. It **does not certify sect admission**.
- **Dormitory assignment:** Staff assign temporary accommodation in the existing East or West Dormitories. Let the player learn the room and any relevant rules in play; do not predetermine roommates or force the protagonists to share a room. Applicants are guests awaiting the decision, not yet enrolled Outer Disciples.
- **Food, rest, and exploration:** Staff explain where to find the Dining Hall and may point out the bathhouse or dormitories. Invite the player to eat, rest, or explore permitted parts of the lower courtyard. Do not rush through these freeform scenes, force a bedtime, or skip meaningful choices.
- **Next-day announcement:** Tell every candidate clearly that **a second examination of spiritual roots, elemental affinities, and cultivation aptitude**, followed by formal acceptance and peak assignment, takes place on **Recruitment Day 2** at **#central-assembly-hall**. The Day 1 trials do not substitute for this crystal-and-formation examination.
- **Access:** Overnight candidates may use the assigned guest facilities but may not claim full disciple privileges, official uniforms, branch membership, or unrestricted access to higher peaks.
