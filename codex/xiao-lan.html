Xiao Lan

Player-safe public reference · 23 October 2026 · Curated snapshot, not a live Notion connection.

🎣 Xiao Lan 13 at recruitment Male Cloudveil applicant

An easygoing street orphan who approaches Cloudveil Sect with a modest—and very understandable—ambition: three meals a day.

Background

Raised on the road by his late father, a beggar, Xiao Lan hopes that life in a cultivation sect might offer shelter, food and a chance to live comfortably. He does not begin his journey with a grand claim to greatness or a promised destiny.

Personality & interests

Laid-back, candid and resourceful, he enjoys fishing, gardening, farming, growing things and good meals. Life on the streets has taught him agility, stealth, pickpocketing and practical survival—ordinary learned skills, not automatic cultivation abilities.

Appearance — arrival at Cloudveil

Age & circumstance: Thirteen, at the Mountain Gate before his first bath and change of clothes.

A thin boy with sun-tanned, dusty limbs and long, untidy black hair usually twisted into a loose bun with an ordinary twig. Heavy bangs obscure his eyes. His patched beige, grey and brown travel clothes show the wear of a difficult life; a small, gently mischievous smile often rests on his face.

Appearance continuity: This describes his arrival, not every later outfit. Age, clothes, disguises, and outward presentation change through confirmed scenes. Personal details concealed in-world are not disclosed by this public introduction.

Starting status

Mortal / Unawakened. The opening recruitment process is underway. A first-day solo adventure has been completed, but Day 2 aptitude testing, formal admission, branch assignment and higher cultivation achievements are not established in this public starting profile.
