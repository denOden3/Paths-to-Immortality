Player character · Recruitment appearance

Xu Yi

Player-safe public reference · 23 October 2026 · Curated snapshot, not a live Notion connection.

🌼 Xu Yi 14 at recruitment Female Cloudveil applicant

A sociable, curious young traveler from a minor noble household, eager to see more of Jianghu and learn about the world.

Background

Xu Yi, sometimes called Ah Yi or Yiyi, is the adopted daughter of a minor noble household. She wishes to join Cloudveil to broaden her knowledge and horizons. The unwritten details of her family and history remain hers to establish through play.

Personality & interests

Friendly, kind and perceptive of social cues, she makes acquaintances easily and is affectionate with people she trusts. She can also overthink and become stressed. She loves reading, singing, cooking, traveling, and discovering interesting curiosities and old objects.

Appearance — recruitment introduction

Age & circumstance: Fourteen, prior to any confirmed formal admission.

Short and chubby, Xu Yi has rounded cheeks, expressive dark eyes, a small pouty mouth and a teardrop-shaped mole beneath her right eye. Her straight, dark, medium-length hair is often worn half-tied or in a bun with a soft yellow ribbon. Comfortable butter-yellow robes are her preferred style, and she wears a teardrop-shaped jade pendant necklace.

Belongings: The jade pendant is an ordinary established accessory; no magical properties are confirmed. Ornamental jade hairpins are a wish, not something she necessarily owns. Her clothing can change as scenes unfold.

Starting status

Mortal / Unawakened applicant. Her spiritual roots and aptitude have not yet been examined in the approved starting record. No Cloudveil branch, formal rank, unexpected abilities or later opportunities have been granted merely by describing them.

Xu Yi is an independent player character. Only her player decides her dialogue, private feelings, choices and long-term ambitions.
