Year 312 · Early Spring · 1st The Mountain Gate Welcomes All

A first day among strangers who became friends

The Mountain Gate Welcomes All

  1. Four strangers at the gate

    Xu Yi met Luo Meifen, Han Dazhu and Qin Ruo while waiting to register. Elder Shen recorded Xu Yi's age, Fengzhou origins and lack of prior cultivation, then issued wooden recruitment token 284.

  2. Across the painted stream

    Instructor Lin's teamwork assessment tasked the four with moving a basket of stones across a damaged crossing. Xu Yi noticed frayed ropes; the group used bamboo, blocks and cooperation to deliver every stone safely.

  3. Taking the mountain together

    The friends completed the mountain ascent at a manageable pace. Xu Yi steadied the height-shy Meifen at a windy, slippery bend, with help from a supervising disciple. Neither was injured.

  4. A room for the night

    The Registration Hall clerk issued provisional lodging slips and Day 2 schedules. Xu Yi, Meifen and Qin Ruo took East Hall Room 6; Xu Yi chose the garden-facing window bed. Dazhu's assignment was West Hall Room 4.

  5. Reading the world around her

    After lunch, Xu Yi explored visitor rules and notices at the ginkgo tree, studied the courtyard map, watched a supervised Practice Square demonstration, and discussed herbs with Meifen in the garden.

  6. Tomorrow's questions

    The group spoke about spiritual-root testing, meridians and their worries, shared dinner, then checked the route to Central Assembly Hall before returning to their dormitory wings.

  7. Goodnight, Yiyi

    Xu Yi left her storybook beside the window bed. The three roommates agreed to wake one another in the morning, joked about Xu Yi's imagined bridge and fell asleep.

End-of-day record

Asleep in the window bed, East Hall Room 6, with Meifen near the door and Qin Ruo by the inner wall. No injuries, admission or cultivation breakthroughs.

Appearance — recruitment introduction

Age & circumstance: Fourteen, prior to any confirmed formal admission.

Short and chubby, Xu Yi has rounded cheeks, expressive dark eyes, a small pouty mouth and a teardrop-shaped mole beneath her right eye. Her straight, dark, medium-length hair is often worn half-tied or in a bun with a soft yellow ribbon. Comfortable butter-yellow robes are her preferred style, and she wears a teardrop-shaped jade pendant necklace.

Belongings: The jade pendant is an ordinary established accessory; no magical properties are confirmed. Ornamental jade hairpins are a wish, not something she necessarily owns. Her clothing can change as scenes unfold.

Background

Xu Yi, also called Ah Yi or Yiyi, is from Fengzhou, the adopted daughter of a minor noble, scholarly household. She came to Cloudveil Sect to learn and broaden her horizons. The unwritten details of her family and history remain hers to establish through play.

Personality & interests

Amiable, kind and attentive to social cues, Xu Yi gets along easily with different people and can be affectionate with those she trusts. She sometimes overthinks and becomes stressed. She enjoys reading, singing, cooking, travel, and exploring interesting curiosities and ancient objects.

People met on Day 1

  • Luo Meifen: Talkative physician's daughter, interested in herbal medicine and afraid of heights. A friend and roommate; Xu Yi helped her on the mountain stairs.
  • Han Dazhu: Enthusiastic, food-minded applicant from a family that repairs agricultural tools. Trial companion and friend; assigned West Hall Room 4.
  • Qin Ruo: Practical, observant girl raised by the river, with dry humor. Friend and roommate in East Hall Room 6.
  • Elder Shen Yizhen: Registered Xu Yi and issued her applicant token.
  • Instructor Lin: Supervised the practical teamwork assessment.

These are confirmed Day 1 encounters. No romance, sworn bond or unplayed personal history is implied.
