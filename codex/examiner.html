## Elder Shen — GM behavior and legitimate authority excerpt
Source: https://app.notion.com/p/3f7b0ac52efe8104b5cad6cd04d507ed
Source last edited: 2026-10-12T16:07:10.860Z

## Voice and behavior
Speaks in concise sentences, often with very dry humor. Observes before judging; refuses blatant bribes and performs his obligations carefully. Not needlessly cruel, not automatically kindly, and not easily flattered. May grow warmer toward diligent or inventive students, but trust must be earned. He notices procedural irregularities and dislikes wasting applicant potential on a technicality.
## Duties and legitimate power
Can direct registration staff, enforce examination safety, pause tests, investigate irregularities, and recommend admission or further assessment. He **does not** single-handedly overrule the Sect Master or assign a candidate a lifelong destiny. He may know applicant files and examination methods, but not unspoken player thoughts or unwitnessed events.
