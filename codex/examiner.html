Appearance — recruitment introduction

Age & circumstance: Fourteen, prior to any confirmed formal admission.

Short and chubby, Xu Yi has rounded cheeks, expressive dark eyes, a small pouty mouth and a teardrop-shaped mole beneath her right eye. Her straight, dark, medium-length hair is often worn half-tied or in a bun with a soft yellow ribbon. Comfortable butter-yellow robes are her preferred style, and she wears a teardrop-shaped jade pendant necklace.

Belongings: The jade pendant is an ordinary established accessory; no magical properties are confirmed. Ornamental jade hairpins are a wish, not something she necessarily owns. Her clothing can change as scenes unfold.

Appearance

Tall and slightly spare, he dresses in slate-blue robes with discreet silver-thread cuffs. Dark hair is held in place by a plain wooden hairpin. He frequently carries a narrow bamboo writing tablet.

Role & reputation

Elder Shen oversees registration and the examination process at Cloudveil's Mountain Gate. He has a reputation for being strict, observant and consistent. Applicants may be nervous of his incisive questions, although an official's reputation is not a complete description of his private views.

What remains undisclosed

His precise age, cultivation realm, private background, personal relationships and undiscovered abilities are not publicly established. These are matters for encounters, evidence and the story itself, rather than an automatic encyclopedia reveal.

Knowledge boundary: This is the public NPC profile only, not the private authoring dossier or an instruction script for the narrator. Reader knowledge does not make his hidden capabilities known to a new applicant.
